# Dedicated branch delivery

**Date:** 2026-11-01  
**Source:** Florent, clarification after completed corrections were left local awaiting commit authorization.

> Florent: « Tu dois commit et push par toi même sur ta branche dédiée à chaque fois et pour valider, tu merges cette branche et tu déploies ce qui n'est pas déployé »

**Settled:** completed changes within an authorized workstream are committed and pushed autonomously on a dedicated branch. Validation merges the branch and deploys authorized missing changes. This does not remove the explicit scope/GO requirement for beginning work, change existing grants or implicitly authorize a version tag/release.
